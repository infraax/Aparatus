# M2 — keep agreements, solo mandate, correction/review, writer lock

Builds on `M1.md`. Same eleven receipt kinds; no new kinds, no scheduler, no SQLite.

## What changed

| Rule | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| K-01 | `queue keep` (create) is refused unless an active `policy {sub_kind: agreement}` covers the asset. Scope entries are exact asset names or `*` (all assets). A policy with `supersedes: <agreement-id>` deactivates that agreement. |
| R-07 | `init --solo` binds continuity, policy, execution **and** mandate_holder to one principal (four `role_bind` receipts). Without `--solo`: continuity only, as in M1. |
| C-06 | `rws correction`: the corrector's role must rank at least the role that signed the corrected object. Rank: continuity 4 > policy = mandate_holder 3 > execution 2 > adviser = coordinator 1. |
| V-01, V-04 | `rws review`: subject must exist; `--out-of-cycle` needs `--trigger <id>`; strategic reviews need `--step 1..6`. |
| K-09 | `--to deferred` is refused unless a `displacement` event on the item was recorded after its last transition. |
| — | Writer lock `.apparatus/LOCK` around every read-check-append. |

## Commands

```bash
# without --solo
apparatus rws init
apparatus rws role-bind --role policy --principal owner
apparatus rws role-bind --role execution --principal owner
apparatus rws role-bind --role mandate_holder --principal owner
apparatus rws policy agreement --asset demo
apparatus rws queue keep --asset demo            # ok
apparatus rws queue keep --asset other           # REFUSED K-01, exit 2, refusal on the chain

# solo
apparatus rws init --solo                        # 4 role_bind receipts
apparatus rws correction --corrects <id> --reason "…" [--replacement <id>] [--removal]
apparatus rws review --kind condition --subject <id>
apparatus rws queue keep --item <id> --to deferred --by <other-item> --reason "…"
apparatus rws check
```

Review kinds: `programme_refresh | condition | account | replica | source_audit | policy_evaluation | quality | strategic`.
Default correction role: the lowest role you hold that still ranks at least the original signer.

## Deferral (K-09): choice made

The **validator** enforces K-09 (so hand-built payloads and `import-jsonl` are covered).
The **CLI** satisfies it for you: `--to deferred` writes two receipts as one unit —
first `event displacement` (subject = the item, `refs` = `--by`, impact = `--reason`),
then the `queue_keep` transition. Both are checked against the evolving state first;
if either would be refused, neither is appended and the refusal is recorded.

## Writer lock

- Every `rws` command takes an exclusive OS advisory lock on `.apparatus/LOCK`
  (`File::try_lock`: `flock` on Unix, `LockFileEx` on Windows) **before** reading the chain,
  and holds it until the process exits. Requires Rust ≥ 1.89.
- A second process retries every 20 ms for up to 2 s, then exits `1` with
  `another apparatus process is writing this project`. Nothing is appended.
- The lock dies with the process, so a crash never leaves a stale lock.
- CAS write and ledger append are still two steps; a crash between them leaves an
  orphan blob (accepted, as in M1). HEAD races are closed.
- Tests: `writer_lock_is_exclusive_and_released_on_drop` (ledger crate) and
  `parallel_ingests_never_corrupt_the_chain` (8 concurrent CLI ingests, then `check`).

## Compatibility with M1 chains

`check` replays history under the current rules. An M1 chain that queued keep work without an
agreement, or deferred without a displacement event, now fails replay (`integrity: … K-01/K-09`).
No migration is shipped; re-create demo projects with the M2 demo above. Rule versioning for
replay is listed in `NOTES/M2-DEFERRED.md`.
